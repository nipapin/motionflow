Hi there,

We found an issue with a recent package update that may have left some assets missing.

A fresh installation will restore the complete 1.3.3 package. Please follow these steps even if your panel already shows 1.3.3.

STEP 1

Remove the installed package

Open your profile in the Gal Toolkit MAX panel. Click the trash icon next to the package.

STEP 2

Confirm removal

In the Remove Package window, click Confirm.

STEP 3

Install the package again

Open your profile again if needed. Click the download icon next to the package and follow the installation steps. Wait for the full download and installation to finish.

Once installation is complete, check that your toolkit assets are available again. If you use the toolkit in both Premiere Pro and After Effects, repeat these steps in each app.

You only need to reinstall the toolkit package. You can keep your existing extension installed.

Interface illustrations use Premiere Pro. Your panel may look slightly different.

Need help? Visit our help section.

Sorry for the inconvenience, and thank you for your patience.
